## [1.0.0] - 2025-05-05

### Initial Release

- Built 6-sheet Excel model: Dashboard, Assumptions, Revenue, UnitEconomics, PnL, Burn & Runway
- 36-month bottom-up MRR build with new customer, churn, and expansion cohort logic
- Full unit economics sheet: LTV, CAC, payback, NRR with benchmark context
- 3-year annual P&L with COGS, S&M, R&D, G&A, EBITDA
- Monthly cash/burn model with opening and closing cash tracking
- Assumption-linked architecture: all blue inputs in one sheet drive all downstream formulas
- Dashboard KPI summary: ARR, LTV:CAC, CAC Payback, Gross Margin, NRR, Burn
